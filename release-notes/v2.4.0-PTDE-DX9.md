# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v2.4.0

Unlocks the frame rate and fixes the physics, camera, timers and HUD that were written for exactly 30 FPS. Files: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`, next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`).

Scripted sequences keep the original game's timing, water reflections at your resolution, the HUD scale through menus and prompts, faster again, and better company for DSfix: DSfix 3.0 by DDTM is now the recommended companion for quality-of-life features.

Everything new is on by default, and every part has an INI switch.

## New in 2.4.0

- **Scripted sequences keep their timing** (`FixEventWaits`). The waits in the game's event scripts (a trap's rhythm, a door, an enemy's scripted attack, a boss's entrance) are counted down by whole frames. At 30 FPS rounding leaves most of them a frame long (0.5 s lasts 0.533 s); at a higher frame rate they ended up to that frame sooner, and long ones drifted. Mostly that cannot be seen, but the Undead Burg's barrel thrower was hit by his own barrel above 30 FPS. Each wait now ends on the frame nearest to where the original game's does, checked for every wait from 0 to 600 s at 30 to 1000 FPS.
- **Water at your resolution** (`WaterResolution = half`). The game draws the scene a pool reflects into a 256x180 picture and its ripples into a 512x360 one. At 4K each pixel of the reflection covered 15x12 of the screen, so the reflection crawled and danced as soon as the camera moved, and the ripples were coarse. Both are now drawn at the game's own ratio to your resolution (at 4K: the reflection 960x540, the ripples 1920x1080), at no measurable cost. `full` draws both twice as fine; `game` keeps the game's sizes.
- **HUD scale through menus and prompts** (`UIScale`). The HUD keeps its size while a menu, a shop or the bonfire menus are open (the shop's rows were pulled into the equipment group before), the windows the game opens over the HUD (prompts, the item pickup window, a message being read) are drawn smaller with it, and the souls just gained stay with the soul counter. The menus themselves keep their size.

## Faster, same picture

- **4 to 6.5% more frames** in seven views measured where the game's draw thread is the limit (Quelaag's Domain 179 to 190 FPS, Blighttown 265 to 282, the Demon Ruins 192 to 204; 1280x720 window, Ryzen 7 5800X3D), and 3 to 4% less render work again where effects fill the view: a scene-sized render target the game made every frame is kept (`ReuseSurfaces`), the game's own sampler-state wrappers answer repeats (`FilterSamplerStates`), and the render thread's matrix paths stay in registers (`FastRenderMath`).
- **`RenderMathPrecision`** (new). `relaxed` (the default) lets render-only math (matrix multiplies, the engine's sine and cosine, the effects' particle loops and curves) round once in float, as a modern compiler would: about a quarter of those results differ in the last binary digit, far below what a pixel can show. Nothing it works out goes back into the game, whose physics, AI and hits stay exact. `exact` keeps every result identical to the original game's.
- **Steam Deck, Linux and phones** (`CacheInputReads`, `auto`: under Wine): the game read the keyboard and mouse four times a frame, and under Wine each read also looked for new input events. They are read once a frame now; on a phone that took the game's input from 3.0 to 1.5 ms a frame.

## DSfix

- **DSfix 3.0 by DDTM is the recommended companion for quality of life**: keyboard and mouse together with a controller, item sorting, a bonfire map, using many items at once, quick save, HUD and camera options and more ([DSfix 3.0 on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/darksouls/mods/1985)). This mod sticks to the frame rate, its fixes, performance and the picture. To use both, set `unlockFPS 0` in `DSfix.ini`, and in `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` `YieldToDSFix = true`, `FixSun = false`, `FixController = false` and `HideMouseCursor = false` (see the README's [DSfix 3.0](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered#dsfix-30)).
- **`FixSun` with DSfix 3.0**: DSfix 3.0 corrects the sun and moon itself, and the mod now sees that and no longer corrects them a second time.
- **`FixWater`** (new): DSfix 2.4 as released in 2015 enlarges the water's ripple buffer with `dofOverrideResolution` while the game keeps reading it as 512x360, so water broke into blocks. The mod now tells the game the buffer's real size. DSfix builds with Durante's later fix, and DSfix 3.0, do that themselves; the mod then does nothing.
- **Credits**: the slide contact, slide steering, cutscene camera and soul counter fixes were first published by DDTM in DSfix 3.0; they are credited in the README and here. The code in this mod was written separately.

**[Prepare to Co-op Edition](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-Prepare-to-Coop-PTDE) 2.0.0** includes FPS Unlock 2.4.0. If you have it, do not install this as well.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy the three files next to the game exe. Upgrading: replace `xinput1_3.dll` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them (replace it too for the new notes). Recommended: exclusive fullscreen with `FullscreenRefreshRate = 0`. Back up your saves. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`; on the Steam Deck keep the game's anti-aliasing off.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

## Known limits

Riding the edge of a short step can leave you stuck against it at a high frame rate. In-engine cutscene cameras are blended; a hard cut still snaps, and pre-rendered movies stay 30 FPS. Steam Deck: the game's anti-aliasing leaves black specks on grass. Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` and any `*-crash.dmp` with bug reports.
