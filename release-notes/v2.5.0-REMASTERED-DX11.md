# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v2.5.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

The effects library's frame-rate bugs, found in Prepare to Die Edition, fixed in Remastered too, and effects that look the way their artists made them, whether they are Remastered's own or the original game's.

## New in 2.5.0

- **Swaying particles** (`FixSfxSway`). Hundreds of effects turn their particles by a random angle every few frames (every 5 in most), and the count went up once a frame, so at 240 FPS they turned four times as often as at 60. They now count the native frames: Remastered's 60 FPS, or the original game's 30 with `SfxSwayPtdeSpeed`.
- **Effect trails keep their length** (`FixSfxTrails`). The ribbons behind sorceries, projectiles and some enemy attacks hold enough points for one a frame at 60 FPS, and almost every trail took a point every frame, so they were cut to half their length at 120 FPS and to a quarter at 240. They now keep their whole length at any frame rate.
- **Particle density** (`FixSfxSpawnRate`, new in Remastered). Remastered's spawn intervals are whole 60 FPS frames (1/30 s for most), and an emitter spawns once its timer reaches the interval, so a frame that came a fraction of a millisecond early made it wait a whole frame more. The timer now fires up to 1/600 s early.
- **Effect looks follow the PTDE effects mod** (`SfxDistortionPtdeSpeed`, `SfxSwayPtdeSpeed`, `SfxTrailsPtdeSpeed`, new default `auto`). Heat haze, swaying particles and trails follow the frame rate their effects were made for: the original game's at 30 FPS; Remastered's artists rebuilt most effects and made them at 60. At startup the mod checks the 29 effect archives in the game's `sfx` folder: with the PTDE effects mod ("Dark Souls Remastered: PTDE Effects Fully Restored") installed, `auto` takes the original game's look, else Remastered's own. The log says what it found (a line starting `Effects:`). `true` and `false` set it by hand.

Heat haze followed the original game's 30 FPS by default up to 2.4.0 (`SfxDistortionPtdeSpeed = true`); with Remastered's own effects it now follows 60 FPS. An INI from an earlier release that lists `SfxDistortionPtdeSpeed = true` keeps the old look: set it to `auto`, or take the new INI.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Upgrading: replace `dinput8.dll`; your INI keeps working. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban, and it starts the game offline by default (`StartOffline`). Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
