# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v2.3.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`). (2.2.0 was a Prepare to Die Edition release; this follows 2.1.0.)

## New in 2.3.0

Three more things that depended on the frame rate keep their timing.

- **Heat haze rolls again** (`FixSfxDistortion`). The haze over lava and fire (the Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, the drake's fire, and the PTDE effects ported to Remastered) moves in real time, so above the native frame rate its pattern changed so little each frame that it shook instead of rolling. It now steps on the native frames: the original game's 30 FPS with `SfxDistortionPtdeSpeed` (default `true`), or Remastered's 60 with `false`.
- **Effects that act at intervals keep their rhythm** (`FixSpEffectTiming`). Lava, heals and other effects that act every 0.1 or 0.2 s rounded each wait up to whole frames, so how often they acted changed with the frame rate. Now they keep one rhythm at any frame rate: the original game's (its 30 FPS frames) with `SpEffectPtdeSpeed` (default `true`), else Remastered's own (60).
- **Bodies loading in no longer fling characters** (`FixVelocity`). 2.1.0 kept the ragdoll's pull toward its pose at the game's strength per update, which at 240 FPS is still four times its strength per second: bodies loading in were pulled to their pose instead of dropping, and characters on them were thrown (Firelink's bodies, the Undead Burg's bandit). It now keeps the game's strength per second.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Upgrading: replace `dinput8.dll`; your INI keeps working. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban, and it starts the game offline by default (`StartOffline`). Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
