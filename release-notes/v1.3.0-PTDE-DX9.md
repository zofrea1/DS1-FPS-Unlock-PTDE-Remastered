# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v1.3.0

Unlocks the frame rate and fixes the physics, camera, timers and HUD that were written for exactly 30 FPS. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` (plus `dxvk_d3d9.dll` for the optional DXVK), next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`). Works with DSfix; set `unlockFPS 0` in `DSfix.ini`.

## New in 1.3.0
- **Particle effects** (`FixSfxSpawnRate`): smoke, sparks, embers and magic spawn as densely as at 30 FPS instead of up to four times as thick at 120. Uses Remastered's own fix for its effect data.
- **Ragdolls and sound** (`FixVelocity`): the powered ragdoll's pull and the Doppler pitch of moving sounds have their original strength.
- **Dropped item glow** fades at the original speed (`FixTimers`).
- **`MaxFPS` 10 to 1000**: values outside the range are clamped, and the game keeps real time down to 8 FPS (was 10). `MaxFPS` still defaults to 120.
- **`FPSUnlock`** is the one master switch: `false` also leaves the fullscreen refresh rate alone.
- **DXVK (optional, off by default)**: `[Renderer] DXVK = true` runs Direct3D 9 on Vulkan (DXVK 3.1.1, included as `dxvk_d3d9.dll`), often with a steadier, higher frame rate. DSfix keeps working on top. If DXVK cannot start, the game runs on Direct3D 9 as usual.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install
Copy the files next to the game exe (`dxvk_d3d9.dll` only if you want DXVK). Recommended: exclusive fullscreen with `FullscreenRefreshRate = 0`. Back up your saves. Well tested with DSfix; other multiplayer is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

## Known limits
Riding the edge of a short step can leave you stuck against it at a high frame rate; in-engine cutscenes can still look like 30 FPS. See the README. Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` and any `*-crash.dmp` with bug reports.
