# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v2.1.0

Unlocks the frame rate and fixes the physics, camera, timers and HUD that were written for exactly 30 FPS. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` (plus `dxvk_d3d9.dll` for the experimental DXVK), next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`). Works with DSfix; set `unlockFPS 0` in `DSfix.ini`.

## New in 2.1.0

Five more things the game counted in frames now keep the original game's 30 FPS timing at any frame rate. At 30 FPS and below nothing changes.

- **Kicks and jumping attacks come out** (`FixStickFlick`). Forward + R1 or R2 needs a quick flick of the stick, which the game measured between two frames. At a high frame rate the flick was rarely seen, so these often came out as plain attacks (a quick push kicked 84% of the time at 30 FPS, never at 240). They now come out as often as at 30 FPS.
- **Rolls go the way you pushed** (`FixRollDirection`). The roll remembers the stick for a few frames, which was only a sixtieth of a second at 240 FPS, so letting go of the stick just before the roll button gave a backstep. The memory is now the original game's length.
- **The switchback plays again** (`FixSwitchback`). Reversing the stick while running plays a quick turn-around when the stick swings far enough within one frame; at a high frame rate it rarely played.
- **Blighttown's swamp poisons at the original rate** (`FixSpEffectTiming`). Effects that are kept up by being applied again came back sooner, so the swamp poisoned up to 60% faster.
- **Lingering attacks hit at the original rate** (`FixBulletHitTiming`). A target standing in Poison Mist, Toxic Mist, Acid Surge, Chaos Storm or similar areas was hit up to 17% more often.
- **Bodies and enemies are no longer flung when they load in** (`FixVelocity`). Since 1.3.0 the ragdoll's pull toward its pose grew with the frame rate, so a body or an enemy loading in could shoot up into a ceiling or through the floor. It now keeps the game's own strength.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet.

**[Prepare to Co-op Edition](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-Prepare-to-Coop-PTDE) 1.7.0** includes FPS Unlock 2.1.0. If you have it, do not install this as well.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy the files next to the game exe (`dxvk_d3d9.dll` only if you want to try DXVK). Upgrading: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working. Recommended: exclusive fullscreen with `FullscreenRefreshRate = 0`. Back up your saves. Well tested with DSfix; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

## Known limits

Riding the edge of a short step can leave you stuck against it at a high frame rate. In-engine cutscene cameras are blended; a hard cut still snaps, and pre-rendered movies stay 30 FPS. Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` and any `*-crash.dmp` with bug reports.
