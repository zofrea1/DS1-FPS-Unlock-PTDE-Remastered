# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v2.4.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 2.4.0

- **Scripted sequences keep their timing** (`FixEventWaits`). The waits in the game's event scripts (a trap's rhythm, a door, an enemy's scripted attack, a boss's entrance) are counted down by whole frames, so how long one really lasts depended on the frame rate: at the native rate rounding leaves most of them a frame long, and at a higher frame rate they ended up to that frame sooner, while long ones drifted. In Prepare to Die Edition this was enough for the Undead Burg's barrel thrower to be hit by his own barrel. Each wait now ends on the frame nearest to where the native countdown ends: the original game's 30 FPS with `EventWaitPtdeSpeed` (default `true`), or Remastered's 60 with `false`, where most waits last their nominal time. Checked for every wait from 0 to 600 s at 30 to 1000 FPS.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet.

The slide contact, slide steering and soul counter fixes were first published for Prepare to Die Edition by DDTM in DSfix 3.0; this mod's ports to Remastered are now credited to it in the README. The code here was written separately.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Upgrading: replace `dinput8.dll`; your INI keeps working. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban, and it starts the game offline by default (`StartOffline`). Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
