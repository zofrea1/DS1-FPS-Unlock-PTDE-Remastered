# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v1.3.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 1.3.0
- **`MaxFPS` 10 to 1000**: a cap below 61 used to switch the mod off without a word, so `MaxFPS = 30` ran the unmodded game at half speed. Values outside the range are now clamped, and the game keeps real time down to 8 FPS (was 20). `MaxFPS` still defaults to 120.
- **Timers** (`FixTimers`, new): the weapon buff glow, the blend into ragdoll, the glow on dropped items and a load queue countdown run at the correct speed.
- **Camera easing** (`FixSmoothing`, new): the follow camera's pivot and look-at point settle at the correct speed.
- **Ragdolls and sound** (`FixVelocity`, new): the powered ragdoll's pull and the Doppler pitch of moving sounds have their correct strength.
- **D-pad hold**, sprint graze, HUD gauges and mouse follow real time at low frame rates too.
- **`FPSUnlock`** is the one master switch: `false` also turns the diagnostics off.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
