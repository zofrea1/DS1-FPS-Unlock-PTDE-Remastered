# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v1.5.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 1.5.0

- **Running jumps at low frame rates** (`FixJump`): at about 30 FPS a running jump could come out about half as long. One take-off frame had no forward motion, and the jump then alternated between stopped and full speed. It now keeps the full take-off speed. The height of the arc is unchanged. At 60 FPS and above those jumps were already full length.
- **Camera after you turn it** (`FixCamera`): for two seconds after you move the camera with the stick or mouse, then for one second as that fades, the camera followed its target several times too fast above 60 FPS. That includes the start of a lock-on. It now takes the same time as at 60 FPS.
- **Ghosts move evenly** (`FixGhosts`): bloodstains and wandering ghosts crossed to the next sample in the first few frames and then stood still. They now move across the whole interval. Turns were already smooth in 1.4.0.
- **Ladder slides at 30 FPS**: sliding down could lift the character instead of dropping them. A real slide is no longer treated as a missed step.
- **`LockOnPtdeSpeed` is on** in the included INI. Lock-on camera pan and body turn use the original game's speed. Set it to `false` for Remastered's faster speed. An older INI that omits the key keeps Remastered's speed.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
