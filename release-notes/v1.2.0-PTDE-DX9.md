# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v1.2.0

Unlocks the frame rate and fixes the physics, camera, timers and HUD that were written for exactly 30 FPS. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Works on the current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`). Loads alongside DSfix; set `unlockFPS 0` in `DSfix.ini`.

## New in 1.2.0
- **Health bar fill** (`FixUI`). The Estus fill and the bar's trailing ghost advanced a fixed amount per frame and finished in 50 ms at 120 FPS; they now take the original time at any frame rate.
- **Loading screen** swirl turns at its original speed.
- **Sprint stamina drain** (`FixStaminaTick`). The game took one stamina point every 0.1 s but rounded each tick up to a whole frame and dropped the remainder, so frame rates that do not divide 0.1 s evenly (for example about 62 FPS) drained slower than intended. It is now 10 a second at any frame rate. Regeneration was already correct.
- **Bonfire softlock watchdog** (`BonfireUnstick`). After "Reverse hollowing" at a bonfire the character could stay seated with no menu open. When that state lasts a second the character is told to stand up. The idea and the addresses come from FPSFix+ by Sean Pesce.
- **Generic HUD gauge smoothing** is scaled by the frame time (part of `FixUI`).
- All diagnostic options in the INI (`Trace`, `InputLog`, `ContentProbe`, `Watch`, `Profile`, `Survey`) stay off.

## Everything from 1.1.0
Real-time speed at any frame rate with `MaxFPS`, the physics fixes (slide, momentum, sprint graze, ladder and ledge snap, timers, smoothing), the lock-on camera fix, `FullscreenRefreshRate` and the optional borderless mode.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. Back up your saves. Well tested with DSfix; other multiplayer is untested, so play offline there. **Recommended: exclusive fullscreen** for the best compatibility with monitors, frame pacing and VRR.

## Known limits
In-engine cutscenes can still look like 30 FPS at a high frame rate; lock-on body turn timing may not match retail exactly at every frame rate. See the README. Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` and any `*-crash.dmp` with bug reports.
