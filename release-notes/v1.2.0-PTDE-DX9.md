# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v1.2.0

Unlocks the frame rate and fixes the physics, camera, timers and HUD that were written for exactly 30 FPS. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`). Works with DSfix; set `unlockFPS 0` in `DSfix.ini`.

## New in 1.2.0
- **Lock-on camera** now pans at the original speed. The look-at point, pivot, distance and other camera blends were still counted per frame, so pans finished early.
- **Lock-on body turn** (`FixLockOnTurn`): your torso, head and hips (and enemies' head and upper body when tracking you) turn at the original speed.
- **Ghost replays** (`FixGhosts`): bloodstains and wandering ghosts play at the original speed, and your own ghost data is recorded at the original rate.
- **Sprint stamina drain** (`FixStaminaTick`): 10 points a second at any frame rate.
- **Bonfire softlock watchdog** (`BonfireUnstick`): stands the character up if it stays seated after "Reverse hollowing". Idea and addresses from FPSFix+ by Sean Pesce.
- **Health bar / Estus fill** and the **loading screen** run at the original speed.
- `FixLadder` is now `FixStepDown`, the same name as in Remastered (the old name still works).

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. Recommended: exclusive fullscreen with `FullscreenRefreshRate = 0`. Back up your saves. Well tested with DSfix; other multiplayer is untested.

## Known limits
Riding the edge of a short step can leave you stuck against it at a high frame rate; in-engine cutscenes can still look like 30 FPS. See the README. Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` and any `*-crash.dmp` with bug reports.
