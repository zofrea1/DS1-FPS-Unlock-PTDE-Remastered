# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v2.9.0

No more flicker where `DrawMapObjects` brought in another room's object, an experimental 32-bit depth buffer under DXVK, and less work for the draw distance's rules. Files: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`, next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`).

## Draw distance

- **No flicker from another room's objects** (`DrawMapObjects`). Some objects are placed for the rooms that see them up close, on top of a coarser map piece that other rooms draw from afar. With `adjacent` and above both were drawn, a few centimetres apart, and flickered as you moved: from the Undead Burg's watchtower, the two spire towers of the building below. Such an object is now left out while that piece is drawn, as the game does from your room. A piece is never left out for an object, and when one room shows both, both stay. Every map was checked for these from the maps' own data (29 objects: the Undead Burg's, and those under Oolacile's far views). At the watchtower, 569 flickering pixels became none in `adjacent`, `adjacent+` and `all`, walking too.
- **Two maps' overlapping pieces kept apart.** Where a map carries a coarse copy of its neighbour, the two versions are now handled as within one map: the coarser drawn a hair behind, or only one drawn where they cross.
- **Less work for the rules** (`DrawMapPieces`): what to hide and what to add is worked out again only when something it depends on changes (the mode, your floor or position, a block loading). It ran every frame, 0.5% of the render thread on the Undead Burg's bridge.

## Depth

- **`ReversedDepth`** (new, experimental, off; needs `DXVK = true`): the depth buffer in 32-bit float, reversed (far 0, near 1), instead of the game's 24 bits. Every vertex shader's depth is mirrored as it is made, with the depth tests, clears and biases; the picture is otherwise the same. The gain far away is small so far, because the mirroring comes after the game's own depth math, which rounds about as coarsely there: at the Undead Burg's spires, 569 flickering pixels became 401 before the fix above. Render thread cost about 0.8%.

## Fixed

- **The "address space low" warning** only appears when the game really has 2 GB. Under Proton (the Steam Deck, Linux) the game has 4 GB without the flag, and the warning wrongly said the 4 GB patch would help.

## Install

Copy the three files next to the game exe. Upgrading from 2.8.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them. Back up your saves. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`.
