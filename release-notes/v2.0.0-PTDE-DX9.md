# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v2.0.0

Unlocks the frame rate and fixes the physics, camera, timers and HUD that were written for exactly 30 FPS. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` (plus `dxvk_d3d9.dll` for the experimental DXVK), next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`). Works with DSfix; set `unlockFPS 0` in `DSfix.ini`.

## New in 2.0.0

The undead dragons' acid now behaves at any frame rate as it does at 30 FPS:

- **It slides as far** (`FixDamping`). A sliding glob is landed again about three times a frame, and each landing's friction and ground factor counted per frame, so at a high frame rate the acid stopped short: standing in one spot, a player was hit at 30 and 60 FPS and missed at 120.
- **Its volleys keep the 30 FPS rhythm** (`FixTimers`). A repeating projectile fires on the frame after its countdown runs out. At a high frame rate that frame is shorter, so the volleys came faster and closer together.
- **It shows on the ground again** (`FixSfxActions`, new, on by default). The acid's part on the ground is spawned one frame after its effect resets its clock, with a count read at that clock: one at 30 FPS, none above it. Effect actions now see the time a 30 FPS frame gives them. Nine of the game's effects have that step, all of them this acid. At 30 FPS nothing changes.

**[Prepare to Co-op Edition](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-Prepare-to-Coop-PTDE) 1.6.0** is built on this release and includes it. If you have it, do not install this as well.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy the files next to the game exe (`dxvk_d3d9.dll` only if you want to try DXVK). Recommended: exclusive fullscreen with `FullscreenRefreshRate = 0`. Back up your saves. Well tested with DSfix; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

## Known limits

Riding the edge of a short step can leave you stuck against it at a high frame rate. In-engine cutscene cameras are blended; a hard cut still snaps, and pre-rendered movies stay 30 FPS. Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` and any `*-crash.dmp` with bug reports.
