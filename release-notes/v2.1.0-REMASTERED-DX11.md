# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v2.1.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 2.1.0

Five more things the game counted in frames now keep their timing at any frame rate.

- **Kicks and jumping attacks come out** (`FixStickFlick`). Forward + R1 or R2 needs a quick flick of the stick, which the game measured between two frames, so at a high frame rate these often came out as plain attacks.
- **Rolls go the way you pushed** (`FixRollDirection`). The roll remembers the stick for a few frames, so at a high frame rate letting go of the stick just before the roll button gave a backstep.
- **The switchback plays again** (`FixSwitchback`). Reversing the stick while running plays a quick turn-around when the stick swings far enough within one frame; at a high frame rate it rarely played.
- **`StickInputPtdeSpeed`** (default `true`) gives those three the original game's 30 FPS timing. Remastered measures them per 60 FPS frame, so it already needed a flick twice as quick as the original game. `false` keeps Remastered's own timing. `StickFlickPtdeSpeed` also works as the key's name.
- **Blighttown's swamp poisons at a steady rate** (`FixSpEffectTiming`). Effects that are kept up by being applied again came back sooner at a high frame rate. `SpEffectPtdeSpeed` (default `true`) uses the original game's rate (30 points a second in the swamp); `false` Remastered's own (36).
- **Lingering attacks hit at a steady rate** (`FixBulletHitTiming`). A target standing in Poison Mist, Toxic Mist, Acid Surge, Chaos Storm or similar areas was hit more often. It follows `BulletPtdeSpeed`.
- **Bodies and enemies are no longer flung when they load in** (`FixVelocity`). Since 1.3.0 the ragdoll's pull toward its pose grew with the frame rate, so a body or an enemy loading in could shoot up into a ceiling or through the floor. It now keeps the game's own strength.
- **The development diagnostics are no longer in the DLL**, as in PTDE 2.0.1: the published DLL is smaller, has no code that pauses threads or reads the keyboard, and has version information (Properties > Details). The INI no longer has a `[Diagnostics]` section; old keys there are ignored.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Upgrading: replace `dinput8.dll`; your INI keeps working. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban, and it starts the game offline by default (`StartOffline`). Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
