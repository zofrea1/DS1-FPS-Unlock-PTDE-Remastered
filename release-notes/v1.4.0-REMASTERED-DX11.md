# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v1.4.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 1.4.0
- **`MaxFPS` below 60 works**: a cap under 60 (for example 30) made the frame pacer wait longer every frame, down to a fraction of a frame per second, even on the intro logos. The cap now holds exactly at any value from 10 to 1000.
- **No more dropped frames when the cap is not held**: when a frame came in one to three frames late, the game skipped the next frame to catch up. With the mod's real-time step this only caused hitches, for example at 30 FPS with `MaxFPS = 60` or a cap set outside the game. Late frames are now shown as they come.
- **Ghosts and other players turn smoothly** (`FixGhosts`): above 60 FPS bloodstain replays, wandering ghosts and other players' characters turned too far between updates and snapped back.
- **Mouse diagnostics**: the log gets a mouse line once a minute while the mouse moves, to help with reports of the camera speeding up. (The camera easing fixed in 1.3.0 is the likely cure for reports made with 1.2.0.)

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
