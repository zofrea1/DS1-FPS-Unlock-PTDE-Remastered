# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v2.7.0 (DRAFT)

Remastered's swaying foliage in Prepare to Die Edition. Files: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`, next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`).

## Foliage sways in the wind, as in Remastered

- **`FoliageSway`** (new, on). Remastered made the grass, flowers, bushes, ferns, ivy and leaves of its maps sway in the wind (Firelink, the Undead Burg, Darkroot Garden, Oolacile, Blighttown and more: 534 models). It drew them with new foliage materials and wind shaders, and re-exported the map models with each blade's sway stored in them. Prepare to Die Edition's models do not have that, so this mod works it out as each map loads: for the same plants Remastered made sway, each blade gets an amplitude from its root to its tip and a phase of its own, by the rule that comes closest to Remastered's own (checked against Remastered's models: Firelink's grass within 2% on average, 84% of all foliage within 15%). The plants then move with Remastered's wind motion and its strength, speed and vertical movement, in every pass (their shadows and reflections sway with them). The wind's direction, timing and strength are the values Remastered's running game gives its wind shaders.
- **`FoliageSwayStrength`** and **`FoliageSwaySpeed`**: in percent of Remastered's (100).
- Cost: about 1% at 4K where foliage fills the view (Firelink 315.7 to 312.5 FPS), nothing measurable elsewhere.

## Fixed

- **Shadows further ahead missing beside DSfix 3.0.** With `ShadowMapResolution` at 4096 (the default) or 8192 and DSfix 3.0 installed, shadows ahead of the player appeared only up close, at a hard line. The game draws its shadow map in four slices, each with a clipping rectangle around it, and DSfix 3.0 did not pass on the rectangles of the three slices beyond the game's own 2048 size, so only the slice nearest the camera was drawn. The mod now puts those rectangles back beneath DSfix. Without DSfix, or with the game's own 2048 map, nothing changes.
- **Flickering surfaces with `DrawMapPieces = adjacent` or `all`.** In nine maps (the Undead Burg and Parish, Anor Londo, Oolacile, Sen's Fortress, Firelink, the Painted World and more) some surfaces exist twice: a detailed piece for the rooms that see it up close and a cheaper stand-in for the rooms that see it from afar. The game never draws both, but `adjacent` and `all` add the pieces of other rooms, so both were drawn in the same place and flickered over each other, depending on where you stood. Those pairs (183, found from the maps' own data) are now checked as you move: the piece your room does not show is hidden while its twin is drawn. Nothing the game itself draws from your room is hidden.
- **Ladder slides as fast as in the original game** (`FixLadderSlide`, new, on). A slide down a ladder is a chain of short animations of 0.5 m each, and each one cost a frame that moved nothing before the next started. At 30 FPS that is one frame in four (3.75 m/s), at 240 FPS one in 25, so at a high frame rate the slide went up to 29% faster (measured on an Undead Burg ladder: 2.53 s to the bottom at 30 FPS, 2.23 at 60, 2.01 at 240). The segments now play slightly slower so the slide takes as long as at 30 FPS at any frame rate (2.50 s at 60, 2.49 at 144, 2.52 at 240), and smoothly, without the 30 FPS pause every fourth frame. At 30 FPS and below nothing changes.
- **Control during Reverse Hollowing at a bonfire.** `BonfireUnstick` stands the character up when it stays seated with no menu (a softlock after Reverse Hollowing at a high frame rate), and it stepped in after one second. Answering the Reverse Hollowing prompt just as the character finished sitting down could leave the seated pose as the character's animation while Reverse Hollowing played, with the menu closed, so the mod stood the character up in the middle of it: the player could walk around while "HUMANITY RESTORED" played, and the bonfire then sat the character back down. Reverse Hollowing and Kindle each take about 6 seconds with the menu closed, so after their prompt the mod now waits until 8 seconds after it: a softlock at the end of either is undone about 1.6 seconds after the character sits again. After any other menu it still steps in after a second.
- **`ShadowMapResolution` with `YieldToDSFix = true`**: the game's own 2048 shadow map, as the rest of the picture is DSfix's then. With `YieldToDSFix = false` beside DSfix, the larger map stays (with the fix above).

## Install

Copy the three files next to the game exe. Upgrading from 2.6.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them. Back up your saves. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`.
