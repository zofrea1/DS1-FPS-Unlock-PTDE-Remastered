# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v2.5.1

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## Fixed

- **`BonfireFix` no longer costs CPU.** Five times a second its watchdog also searched the player's data for every place the seated animation showed up (left over from finding the one place it reads), checking each memory page before reading it. That took most of a CPU core whether you were at a bonfire or not (88% of a core, measured with 2.4.2 at 60 FPS), so the CPU ran busier and hotter. It now reads only the animation and the menus' open flags: about 0.003% of a core.
- **`BonfireFix` waits out Reverse Hollowing and Kindle.** It stands the character up when it stays seated at a bonfire with no menu open, and it stepped in after one second. Reverse Hollowing and Kindle each play about 6 seconds with the menu closed, and in Prepare to Die Edition the same rule once stood a character up in the middle of Reverse Hollowing. It now also waits until no menu or prompt has been open for 8 seconds.

## New

- **Ladder slides at the same speed at any frame rate** (`FixLadderSlide`, on). A slide down a ladder is a chain of short animations of 0.5 m each, and each one cost a frame that moved nothing before the next started, so above 60 FPS the slide got faster the higher the frame rate (4.3 m/s at 60 FPS, 4.8 at 240). Its segments now play slightly slower so the slide takes as long as at 60 FPS, smoothly. At 60 FPS and below nothing changes. Measured in Prepare to Die Edition, which has the same slide.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Upgrading: replace `dinput8.dll`; your INI keeps working. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban, and it starts the game offline by default (`StartOffline`). Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
