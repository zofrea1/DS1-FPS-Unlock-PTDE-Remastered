# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v2.10.0

Lips and ledges climbed as at the original frame rate, and no more enemies carried up walls. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

Remastered carries the same version number as Prepare to Die Edition again: 2.8.0 and 2.9.0 were Prepare to Die Edition releases only, so this follows 2.7.0.

## New

- **Lips a little above a step are climbed as at the original frame rate** (`FixStepUp`, on): 60 FPS, or 30 FPS with `StepHeightPtde`. A lip a little higher than the step height is climbed by creeping up its edge, and the short frames of a high frame rate crept far less: the Great Hollow's Chloranthy Ring hollow could not be left. A character that is pushing against something but hardly moving is now moved as at that rate: every frame of it, its frames are moved again as one. Measured in Prepare to Die Edition, which has the same movement code: that lip takes 0.27 to 0.38 s at any frame rate from 45 to 240 FPS, as at 30, and what 30 FPS cannot climb stays unclimbable. Enemies get it too.

## Fixed

- **Enemies no longer carried up walls** (`FixStepDown`). A character pressed against a wall could rise a step at a time: the game's ground check started inside the wall and reported ground above the character, so the frame's step lift was never taken back. In Prepare to Die Edition one of the Lower Undead Burg's door-ambush thieves shot 12 to 16 m into the sky at 120 FPS. Above 60 FPS (30 with `StepDownPtde`) such a frame now keeps the character at its height.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet. Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Upgrading from 2.7.0: replace `dinput8.dll`; your INI keeps working. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban, and it starts the game offline by default (`StartOffline`). Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
