# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v3.1.0

The in-game settings page comes to Remastered, and NPCs place their feet on stairs and slopes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New

- **FPS Unlock Settings** (`SettingsMenu`, `SettingsMenuKey`, on): a page in the game with the settings you may want to change while playing. Open it with F1, or with Y on a controller anywhere in the game's System menu (its list and every screen in it, on the title screen too; a line at the bottom says so). Up/down chooses a row, left/right changes it, LB/RB (or Q/E) turns the page, B or Esc closes it.
  - **Frame rate**: the frame rate cap, real time when the frame rate dips, menu presses counted once, standing up from a stuck bonfire, starting offline, skipping the start-up logos.
  - **Movement**: for each movement switch, the original game's feel or Remastered's (lock-on turn speed, sliding off a slope, walls and corners, ground that drops away, step height, momentum in the air, feet on stairs and slopes, slides, stick timing), and NPCs' feet on the ground.
  - **Timing**: the same for the HUD bars, poison and other effects, scripted waits, crawling and repeating attacks, heat haze, swaying particles and trails.

  Each change is written to the INI at once; most apply at once, and the page says which need a restart. The fixes stay in the INI. The page is drawn in the game's own frames and was tested beside ReShade. `SettingsMenu = false` turns it off and then also leaves the game's DXGI factory and swap chain alone, for an overlay that does not get on with it.
- **NPCs' feet on stairs and slopes** (`FootIkNpcs`, on). The game places the feet of your own character only, so human NPCs and summoned phantoms stood on stairs, slopes and roofs with the feet of their animation for flat ground. They now place their feet as your character does. Other players' characters are left to the game.

All new switches are on by default, also when your INI does not list them yet. Details are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. Upgrading from 3.0.0: replace `dinput8.dll`; your INI keeps working. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory; using it on official servers could risk a ban, and it starts the game offline by default (`StartOffline`). Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
