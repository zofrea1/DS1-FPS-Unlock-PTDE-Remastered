# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v1.2.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 1.2.0
- **Lock-on camera** now pans at the correct speed. The look-at point, pivot, distance and other camera blends were still counted per frame, so pans finished early.
- **Lock-on body turn** (`FixLockOnTurn`): your torso, head and hips (and enemies' head and upper body when tracking you) turn at the correct speed.
- **`LockOnPtdeSpeed`** (was `CameraPtdeSpeed`): the original game's lock-on speed for both the camera and the body turn. Off by default.
- **Ghost replays** (`FixGhosts`): bloodstains and wandering ghosts play at the correct speed, and your own ghost data is recorded at the correct rate for other players.
- **Sprint graze** matches the game exactly at every frame rate.
- **Sprint stamina drain** (`FixStaminaTick`): 10 points a second at any frame rate.
- **D-pad hold** (`FixDpadHold`): no longer fires on an ordinary tap.
- **Health bar / Estus fill** and the **loading screen** run at the correct speed.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.
