# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v1.2.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Works on the current Steam build only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`, 1.03.1).

## New in 1.2.0
- **Sprint stamina drain** (`FixStaminaTick`). The game took one stamina point every 0.1 s, but rounded each tick up to a whole frame and dropped the remainder. The drain therefore ran slower the further the frame time was from a clean divisor of 0.1 s: 8.7 points a second at 61 FPS, 9.5 at 120, 9.7 at 240. It is now 10 a second at any frame rate. Stamina regeneration was already correct.
- **D-pad hold** (`FixDpadHold`). Holding D-pad down jumps back to the first quick item after a short hold, but the game counted the hold in frames (15), so at a high frame rate it fired during an ordinary tap. The hold now takes a quarter second at any frame rate.
- **Loading screen** now turns at the correct speed from the very first load after start-up.
- **HUD gauges** (`FixUI`). The health bar's fill animation and the generic gauge smoothing are scaled by the frame time (a no-op at 60 FPS).
- The INI keeps every diagnostic option (`Trace`, `Watch`, `InputLog`) off. Nothing in the release writes extra logs or slows the game.

## Everything from 1.1.0
Variable frame time with `MaxFPS`, the physics fixes (slide, momentum, sprint graze, ladders, ledges), the lock-on camera fix and the crash and start-up hardening.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. Set the display frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync to off in the game's settings, then restart. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; other multiplayer is untested, so play offline there. **Recommended: borderless fullscreen** for the best compatibility with monitors, frame pacing and VRR.
