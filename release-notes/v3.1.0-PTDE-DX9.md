# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v3.1.0

Anti-aliasing that no longer depends on the game's option, NPCs' feet on stairs and slopes, swaying trees (contributed, off by default), the settings page from anywhere in the System menu and on the Steam Deck, and Darkroot's popping bushes fixed. Files: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`, next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`).

## Picture

- **Anti-aliasing switched by the mod** (`AntialiasingSamples`). It followed only the game's Antialiasing option, which players who used DSfix have usually left off (DSfix needs it off): then the picture had none at all, every edge stair-stepped and thin bright edges broke into dashes, whatever `AntialiasingSamples` said. `2`, `4` or `8` now switch it on by themselves, `off` switches it off, and `game` leaves it to the game's option as before. Beside DSfix it stays off while DSfix makes the resolution. **With the default `2`, a game whose own option is off now gets 2x anti-aliasing** (set `game` for the old behaviour). On a phone the game's option still decides (`PhoneAntialiasingSamples = game`). Off and on change at once on the settings page.
- **Swaying trees** (`FoliageTreeSway`, new, **off**; a contributed extra Remastered does not have): with swaying foliage on, the leaves of the trees around Firelink Shrine, in the Undead Burg and Parish, on the way up to Sen's Fortress and in Anor Londo sway in the wind; trunks and branches stay still. Switched at once on the settings page ("Swaying trees").

## Characters

- **NPCs' feet on stairs and slopes** (`FootIkNpcs`, new, on). The game places the feet of your own character only, so human NPCs and summoned phantoms stood on stairs, slopes and roofs with the feet of their animation for flat ground, floating above it or sinking into it. They now place their feet and lower their hips as your character does. About 0.015 ms per character on screen (0.1 ms for seven NPCs).

## Draw distance

- **Fixed: bushes and flowers popping on Darkroot Garden's cliff path** (`DrawMapPieces = adjacent+`). A layer of grass, flowers and bushes was taken for another room's coarser version of the same place and hidden from the rooms beside it, so it appeared and vanished as you crossed between them. Foliage no longer counts in that comparison.

## FPS Unlock Settings

- **Y opens the page anywhere in the System menu**: its list and every screen in it, not only the PC settings screen (a line at the bottom says so). F1 works as before.
- **Fixed: no page on the Steam Deck.** With Proton's own DXVK (`DXVK = false`, the Deck's usual setup) the page, its hint and Y never showed: the game's frames were not watched in that mode. They are now in every mode.

## Install

Copy the three files next to the game exe. Upgrading from 3.0.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll` (`dxvk_d3d9.dll` is unchanged); your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them. Note the anti-aliasing change above if you play with the game's own option off. Back up your saves. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`.
