# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v1.9.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 1.9.0

- **Steep slides keep their footing** (`FixSlideContact`): the slide's ground flag is kept until it has been missing for one native frame, so one short missed frame at the lip does not cancel the slide. At the native rate and below nothing is held.
- **Steering on a slide** (`FixSlideSteer`): a slide's turn restarts every frame, and Havok was throwing out most of that one-frame-old root motion, so the legs turned and the body barely moved, the less the higher the frame rate. The threshold follows the frame time. Remastered kept the original game's animation blend times at twice the rate, so out of the box its slide turn has a quarter of the original game's weight.
- **`SlidePtdeSpeed`** (default true) picks which slide the two fixes reproduce at any frame rate: the original game's (true) or Remastered's own 60 FPS slide (false).
- **Soul counter** (`FixSoulCounter`): the on-screen count of a soul pickup follows the frame time instead of finishing several times too fast. It runs at the original game's speed with `HudPtdeSpeed` on and at Remastered's with it off. A hitch adds at most four steps.
- Prepare to Die Edition also gets the FPS cap point (`FPSLimitPoint`), `SkipIntro` on its own switch and the in-engine cutscene camera blend; none of these is needed in Remastered.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.
