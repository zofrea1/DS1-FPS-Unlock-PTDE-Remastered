# Dark Souls: Remastered, FPS Unlock v1.9.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `dinput8.dll` and `DSR-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsRemastered.exe`. Steam build 1.03.1 only (SHA-1 `9150CC63C617332ED3C2C66E7566ED67E3292DA0`).

## New in 1.9.0

- **Steep slides keep their footing** (`FixSlideContact`): the slide's ground flag is held for 1/30 s after the last real contact, so one short missed frame at the lip does not cancel the slide. At 30 FPS and below it still drops on the next frame.
- **Steering on a slide** (`FixSlideSteer`): a slide's turn restarts every frame, and Havok was throwing out that one-frame-old root motion, so the legs turned and the body barely moved. The threshold follows the frame time. At 30 FPS and below it is unchanged.
- **Soul counter** (`FixSoulCounter`): the on-screen count of a soul pickup follows the frame time instead of finishing several times too fast. It runs at the original game's speed unless `HudPtdeSpeed` is off. A hitch adds at most four steps.
- Prepare to Die Edition also gets the FPS cap point (`FPSLimitPoint`), `SkipIntro` on its own switch and the in-engine cutscene camera blend; none of these is needed in Remastered.

Details for every fix are in the README.

## Install

Copy both files next to the game exe. In the game's display settings set Frequency to your monitor's rate and Vertical sync off, then restart. Recommended: borderless fullscreen. Back up your saves. Well tested with Seamless Co-op; official online play is untested.
