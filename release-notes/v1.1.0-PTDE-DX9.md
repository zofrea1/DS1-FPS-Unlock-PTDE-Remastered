# Dark Souls: Prepare to Die Edition, FPS Unlock v1.1.0

First release for PTDE: unlocks the frame rate and fixes the physics, camera and timers that were written for exactly 30 FPS. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DARKSOULS.exe`. Works on the current Steam build only (`SizeOfImage` `0x11C2000`). Loads alongside DSfix; set `unlockFPS 0` in `DSfix.ini`.

## What it does
- **Real-time at any frame rate.** The measured frame time is written into the game's fixed 1/30 step every frame, the rate is capped at `MaxFPS` (default 120), and the render thread stops waiting two vertical blanks per frame.
- **Physics fixes**, each with an INI switch: slope slide and friction (`FixSlide`), airborne momentum (`FixDamping`), sprint slowdown when grazing a wall or running at a high frame rate (`FixGraze`), ladder slide and ledge walk-off ground snap (`FixLadder`), per-frame timers (`FixTimers`) and interpolation factors (`FixSmoothing`).
- **Lock-on camera** (`FixCamera`). Target switches and edge lock-ons pan at the original speed instead of snapping.
- **Display.** `FullscreenRefreshRate` asks for your display's real refresh rate in exclusive fullscreen (the game offers only 59/60 Hz); `BorderlessFullscreen` is optional.
- **Diagnostics.** A heartbeat every 30 seconds, a stall watchdog and a minidump on crash.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. Back up your saves. Well tested with DSfix; other multiplayer is untested, so play offline there. **Recommended: exclusive fullscreen** for the best compatibility with monitors, frame pacing and VRR.

## Known limits
In-engine cutscenes can still look like 30 FPS at a high frame rate; lock-on body turn timing may not match retail exactly at every frame rate. See the README. Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` and any `*-crash.dmp` with bug reports.
